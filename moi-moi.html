Moin Moin

A savoury steamed bean pudding that works as a side dish or a meal on its own.

Video tutorial

Watch the tutorial to guide you as you prepare the ingredients.

Ingredients

Here are the list of the ingredients that you will be using. Ensure to get them ready

  • 2 cups peeled black-eyed beans or honey beans
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper, optional
  • 1 medium onion
  • ¼ cup vegetable oil
  • 1½ cups water or stock, as needed
  • 2 boiled eggs or flaked fish (optional)
  • 1 seasoning cube (optional)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Heat-safe moi moi cups, leaves or foil containers
