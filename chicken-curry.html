Chicken Curry

A warming, gently spiced chicken curry with a creamy sauce, perfect with rice.

Video tutorial

Watch the tutorial to guide you as you prepare the ingredients.

Ingredients

Here are the list of the ingredients that you will be using. Ensure to get them ready

  • 500 g boneless chicken, cubed
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tablespoons curry powder or garam masala
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped or 1 can chopped tomatoes
  • 200 ml coconut milk or cream
  • ½ cup water or chicken stock
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Fresh coriander, to serve
  • Steamed rice or naan, for serving
