Egusi Soup

A hearty melon-seed soup with leafy greens, peppers and your choice of protein

Video tutorial

Watch the tutorial to guide you as you prepare the ingredients.

Ingredients

Here are the list of the ingredients that you will be using. Ensure to get them ready

  • 2 cups ground egusi (melon seeds)
  • 4 medium tomatoes, blended
  • 1 cup smoked fish, deboned
  • 1 small piece stockfish, softened (optional)
  • ½ cup palm oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons ground crayfish
  • 2 cups blended tomatoes and peppers
  • 2–3 cups chopped ugu or spinach
  • 2 cups meat stock or water
  • Salt and seasoning cubes, to taste
