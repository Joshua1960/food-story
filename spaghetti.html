Spaghetti Bolognese

A comforting Italian-inspired pasta dish with a rich tomato and beef sauce.

Video tutorial

Watch the tutorial to guide you as you prepare the ingredients.

Ingredients

Here are the list of the ingredients that you will be using. Ensure to get them ready

  • 300-350 g spaghetti
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 400-500 g minced beef
  • 1 carrot, finely diced (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 can (400 g) chopped tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Grated Parmesan, to serve
  • Fresh basil or parsley, optional
